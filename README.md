I learned data analysis by watching Alex Freberg YouTube videos. As a proud graduate of his "School of YouTube," I thought, what better way to end the month than by analyzing his YouTube channel?

# Goals of the Analysis:
-Identify the tools and topics viewers are learning from his videos.
-Highlight the most popular videos.
-Determine his busiest year, quarter, and month for content creation.
-Calculate total views across all his videos.

# The Process:
-Data Collection: Scraped video data (title, publishing date, and views) using Python and imported it into Power BI.
-Data Preparation: Used DAX functions to extract categories and tools mentioned in the video titles.
-Visualization: Designed interactive visuals to explore and present insights.
